ONE VIEW OF THE WORK

Nothing open falls through.

01

To review

Direct requests and requests to every visible GitHub team you belong to, kept together and clearly attributed.

02

Involved

Open PRs you committed to, reviewed, commented on, were assigned to, or were mentioned in.

03

Your PRs

Every open PR you authored—including drafts and PRs with no reviews—alongside the effective review state.

04

Every state

Approvals, comments, requested changes, dismissed and pending reviews, review required, and no reviews yet.

05

Built for flow

Keyboard and mouse navigation, browser opening, branch and PR URL copying, rate-aware background refresh, and stable selection.

06

Your GitHub access

Kritikon reuses your authenticated GitHub CLI session. It never reads or stores your token itself.
